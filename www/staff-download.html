How to install

  1. Tap the button above to download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn that it is from an unknown source — tap Settings and allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open Aapla Swad Staff.
  4. Log in with your phone number and PIN. Allow notifications when asked, so you hear new orders.

Staff only. You need an account created by the owner to log in.
Works on Android. iPhone is not supported yet.
